BarSpeed MVP

Video-based barbell velocity estimator — prototype

1. Film correctly

  1. Phone stationary, side-on to the bar path.
  2. Use a standard 450 mm Olympic plate if possible.
  3. Put a bright, unique marker/tape patch on the visible plate or sleeve.
  4. 60 fps works; 120/240 fps is preferable for fast lifts.

3. Calibrate distance

Pause on a frame where the plate is clearly visible. Tap opposite edges of the 450 mm plate. The app converts pixels to metres.

Load a video and grab a frame.

4. Select marker

After calibration, tap the center of your bright marker. Tracking works best when its color is unlike skin, plates, clothing and the background.

No marker selected.

5. Analyze

Rewind to just before the set, then analyze. Keep the entire marker visible. Processing occurs on your device.
